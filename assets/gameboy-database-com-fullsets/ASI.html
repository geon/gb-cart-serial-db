ASI (for ASIan) is the code used for distribution in Malaysia, Indonesia and Singapore. The only company allowed to distribute ASI games was UPTRON, a company that is still going today and is also currently the official distributor of Playstation in Singapore ! It is worth noting that these games were sold during a short period of time, at the very beginning of the life of the Game Boy, and as such ASI games are some of the earliest games released.
Several ASI games have been exported to Europe.
