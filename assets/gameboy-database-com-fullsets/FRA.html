FRA (for FRAnce) is the code used for distribution in France. FRA distributors were roughly speaking the same as for the FAH localization sales in France. These FRA games are mainly games that were translated into French, even if there are exceptions. lt's worth noting that Yannick Noah Tennis is not a FRA exclusive game, as it is in fact a reissue of Jimmy Connors Tennis.
Finally, it is interesting to note that Hercules exists with a NFRA box, but with a NOE cartridge (and not a FRA cartridge).
