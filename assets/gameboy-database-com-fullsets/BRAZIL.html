Starting from 1993, Playtronic was the company in charge of the official distribution of the Game Boy games on the Brazilian soil. Gradiente bought Playtronic in 1996, and then took over the responsibility of distributing the games, till the end of the Game Boy. Playtronic and Gradiente games are quite difficult to find, and even if we dug deeply this localization with other collectors, it is not impossible we may have missed some games. There is no exclusive Brazilian game. Thus, it is interesting to point that a few games had their name translated, as the Bugs Bunny Crazy Castle games that became Pernalonga.
