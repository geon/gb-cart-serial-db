FAH (for France And Holland) is the code used for distribution in France, Belgium, Luxembourg and Netherlands. There were a lot of different distributors for this FAH localization; one single game could be distributed by different distributors according to the country. Some of the most important FAH distributors were BANDAI and Guillemot International, a division of Ubisoft.
FAH games were widely distributed in the four countries. A few of these FAH games were exported to neighbouring countries such as Germany or ltaly, but this was a negligible occurance. There are no FAH exclusive games, but when it came to the FAH versions of Lethal Weapon L'Arme Fatale and The New Chessmaster, these were the only European releases of these games.
Note that several versions of FAH games were never sold in France, but only in Belgium or in the Netherlands.
